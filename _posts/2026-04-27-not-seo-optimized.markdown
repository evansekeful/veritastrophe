My blog, VERITAStrophe, is not SEO optimized and that’s okay. It is also not tailored to extract your money and attention.

I originally conceived of my blog in 2014 at first to teach myself how to use WordPress and make a first pass attempt at personal branding. My high school English teacher once said that I had a distinct voice, and I’ve carried that quietly as a badge of pride for over 20 years now. As for the fate of the original incarnation of my blog, life simply got in the way. My hosting bill lapsed. I went back to school and got a fancy corporate job that probably would prefer I not have a distinct voice at my current career level. One morning after a night of no sleep in 2023 I had even attempted to restart my amateur writing career on the WordPress.com platform and have not touched it since.

Now after years of struggling with self-care and work-life boundaries I want my voice back. I have a journal that I keep but I miss the spirit of the internet I remember as an aging Millennial with a capital “M.” I want to share and learn in public. And after being somewhat coerced into transitioning my 20\+ year-old Facebook profile into a public figure account I realized I actually don’t care about monetization. It feels icky and I don’t want to feel that way about something I do for fun. I have a lot of thoughts and opinions, and I would rather my livelihood depend on the ones I don’t care as much about.

I am not my job and my blog is just me. And the stakes of being imperfect are much lower without a paycheck attached to it. I could probably write paragraphs on how the monetary incentives of generating content have done harm to our society, but I have nothing to say that hasn’t already been said. Rehashing the same old arguments is also something I’m not particularly fond of; I really need to emphasize here that I write for me. And this is why my blog isn’t SEO optimized and that’s okay.
